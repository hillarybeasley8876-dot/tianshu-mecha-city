# mech-move — numeric-robustness audit (lead, 2026-11-02) — 7 verified findings

A 36-agent audit read every non-test script twice (non-finite math; clocks + integrators) and a skeptic
tried to refute each finding; these survived. Fix them all (the proposed fixes were checked by the
skeptic — where it corrected one, the correction is what is written under *fix*). Add a regression check
for each high one in your suite, keep every suite green, run one scripted fight with `--nan-watch`, commit,
and report per finding: fixed (how) / not reproducible (evidence).

Clock contract reminder (CONTRACTS §3): Feel.real_delta/real_now for input, camera, UI/post and rate
limits; Feel.player_delta for player timers/anims; plain delta for world VFX; NEVER the wall clock
(movie capture renders a frame in ~70 ms of wall time). Feel now divides _process deltas by the
frame's own scale, so Feel.real_delta(delta) is correct in _process too.

## 1. [high] game/player/mech/mech_locomotion.gd:176 (other)
**What**: Out of lens (the area is clean for non-finite math): face_toward computes turn = angle_difference(target, current), which is current minus target, then rotate_y(+turn), so the body's -Z ends up pointing away from `dir`. This affects every caller (ground run, air steer, boost/assault skate via MechBoost._steer, flight, strafe, lunge, locked turn). Perfect alignment is an unstable equilibrium, and the stable state is facing backwards with a ±1-2° flip every tick.

**Trigger**: Any steering. I ran this exact expression on Godot 4.7.2: target (-1,0,0) ends at forward (1,0,0); target (0.6,0,0.8) ends at (-0.588,0,-0.809); a 1e-6 rad offset from alignment grows to a 107° turn in 1 s. Holding move_back from rest keeps the mech facing forward while it moves backwards. captures/mech-move_r3_t1_5.jpg shows the hero's front facing the camera while it runs north, away from it. Knock-on effects: MechDash.label reports dash_b for forward dashes, and facing() / the look_dir fallback point backwards for the combat units.

**Fix**: Swap the arguments: `var turn := angle_difference(atan2(f.x, f.z), atan2(dir.x, dir.z))`. That is target minus current, the same convention MechBoost._steer gets right with rotated(UP, -turn). I verified in the same Godot probe that it converges to `dir`. Correct the comment, and add a test that runs in a turned direction and asserts (-global_basis.z).dot(wish) > 0.99.

**Skeptic's check**: angle_difference(from, to) returns to - from, so mech_locomotion.gd:176 computes turn = atan2(f) - atan2(dir). rotate_y(+t) adds t to atan2(f.x, f.z), because forward = (-sin a, 0, -cos a) gives atan2 = a + PI, which moves the same way as a, not the opposite way the comment claims. I ran the exact expression in a Godot 4.7.2 scratch project. Target (-1,0,0) ends with forward (1,0,0). Target (0.6,0,0.8) ends with (-0.589,0,-0.808). A 1e-6 rad offset grows to 106.9 deg in 1 s. Target (0,0,1) from rest stays at forward (0,0,-1). With the swapped arguments it converges (dot = 1.0). Every caller is affected, including MechBoost._steer (mech_boost.gd:56). The tests never check facing; _test_run_speed starts exactly aligned, which is the one equilibrium that holds.

## 2. [high] game/player/mech/player_mech.gd:179 (integrator)
**What**: During a local hit-stop, MechLocomotion.tick() returns before any state handler runs (mech_locomotion.gd:81-83) and post_move() is skipped (mech_locomotion.gd:100-101), but PlayerMech still calls move_and_slide() every tick, so the 'frozen' mech keeps moving at its pre-pause velocity with gravity, braking, the lunge arrival/wall stop and the dash jet clock (_dash_t) all stopped, and any floor-contact change during the pause is never processed.

**Trigger**: Feel.local_hit_stop([player, ...]) from a melee hit (melee_strike.gd:49, 4-10 f), a perfect parry (defense_system.gd:264, 8 f) or the first missile of every salvo (missile_manager.gd:198, 0.05 s) while the mech moves. A lunge hit landing before the arrival stop carries the mech on at 110 m/s for the 7 f 'freeze' (up to about 13 m into or around the target). A salvo hit during a quick boost holds the 150->42 m/s jet speed for 6 ticks while _dash_t stands still, so the 24 m dash grows by up to about 7.5 m and the i-frames last longer. A falling mech that touches down inside the pause never runs MechContact.touchdown: on the next tick _on_floor is already true, so it stays in S.AIR on the floor ('fall' pose, jump only refills _jump_buffer, a double tap enters and immediately leaves flight) until a dash, boost or attack resets it.

**Fix**: Apply the scoped fix as written: is_frozen() = _pause_t > 0 and state in {LOCKED, LUNGE}, and skip the move while frozen. Also run post_move's contact detection unconditionally as a safety net, and forward the pause to the model (see [4]).

**Skeptic's check**: This is the same root cause as [1], and the code proves it. While _pause_t > 0, no state handler runs (mech_locomotion.gd:81-83). That means none of these run: MechDash.dash's `_dash_t`/`_dash_iframes_t` advance (mech_dash.gd:58-59), the lunge arrival and head-on stop (mech_air.gd:199-204), gravity, or braking. Meanwhile player_mech.gd:179 keeps moving the body at the last velocity. A salvo hit (0.05 s, 6 ticks) during a 150 m/s jet adds up to about 7.5 m, and is_dash_invulnerable stays true for longer. A 7 f lunge hit-stop carries the body on at 110 m/s until the enemy collider (Layers.MASK_CHARACTER includes ENEMY) stops it or deflects it. The missed touchdown leaves the mech stuck in S.AIR, as described in [1]. The proposed scoped fix (freeze only LOCKED/LUNGE and skip the move then) matches FEEL.md.

## 3. [medium] game/player/mech/mech_locomotion.gd:78 (clock)
**What**: input.poll() runs before the `_pause_t > 0` early return, so a jump/boost/assault rising edge that arrives during a local hit-stop is consumed (the _prev_* flags update) but no state ever reads it; the press is lost instead of buffered, against juice section 2.2 'buffer inputs pressed during a stop'.

**Trigger**: Tap boost (dash), jump or assault within the 0.05 s after a missile salvo's first impact, or within the 0.133 s of a perfect parry: nothing happens. If boost is still held when the pause ends, the intended dash becomes a slide. A jump eaten in the pause still sets _last_jump_press, so the next press within 0.30 s reads as a double tap and toggles flight unexpectedly.

**Fix**: Latch the edges while paused: OR jump_pressed/boost_pressed/assault_pressed (and double_jump) into pending flags that the first unpaused tick consumes and clears. Skipping input.poll() while _pause_t > 0 also works for presses still held at the end of the pause, but it still loses a tap shorter than the pause. Limiting the pause to LOCKED/LUNGE (previous finding) removes the problem for player-started states.

**Skeptic's check**: mech_locomotion.gd:78 polls before the pause check at :81. MechInput.poll updates _prev_jump, _prev_boost and _prev_assault (mech_input.gd:49/55/60), so a rising edge that occurs during the pause is consumed and no state reads it. A boost still held after the pause is seen only as boost_held, so GROUND (mech_locomotion.gd:246) and AIR start a slide instead of a dash. Every melee hit pauses the player for 4-10 f (melee_strike.gd:49), so a dash cancel timed on the hit is the usual trigger. The double-tap sub-claim is weak: in AIR, two presses within 0.30 s really are a double tap, and GROUND ignores double_jump. The main claim stands and contradicts juice section 2.2, which says to buffer inputs pressed during a stop.

## 4. [medium] game/player/mech/mech_stepping.gd:74 (integrator)
**What**: try_hop solves the launch speed v0 for a fall_gravity (25.5 m/s^2) arc at constant horizontal speed. When the hop fires from S.ASSAULT, post_move() (mech_locomotion.gd:109-110) demotes the now-airborne mech to S.AIR, whose _vertical()/apply_gravity integrates the rise with rise_gravity (79 m/s^2) and whose _steer brakes toward 16 m/s, so the solved arc is never flown. (A slide hop survives only because AIR re-enters SLIDE on the next tick while boost is held.)

**Trigger**: Assault boost in normal toggle use (boost released) driven into any 3-7 m block. Replaying the code's own integrators at 60-84 m/s puts the capsule bottom 0.1-0.7 m below the top when it reaches the face, where the solve expects +1.1 m. The mech clips the edge and 'lands' on it: touchdown takes |upward vy| as the impact, giving a LAND recovery with braking and the land_light feel instead of a clean vault. The jet also dies mid-hop, because AIR ignores assault_on.

**Fix**: Keep the airborne ASSAULT in S.ASSAULT, as proposed, so MechBoost.assault's airborne branch flies the solved arc. That alone is incomplete. MechContact.touchdown still sets assault_on = false and enters LAND on the hop's landing (mech_contact.gd:14, 30-39), and with the 6 m/s glide_sink cap the impact is often >= 5. So the jet still dies one landing later. Use impact = maxf(-_pre_vy, 0.0), and do not drop assault_on or enter LAND when an ASSAULT or SLIDE touches down from a hop, for example while _hop_cd > 0.

**Skeptic's check**: try_hop solves v0 for fall_gravity and constant speed (mech_stepping.gd:73-74). post_move then demotes the airborne ASSAULT to S.AIR (mech_locomotion.gd:109-110). There, apply_gravity uses rise_gravity 79 while vy > 0 (:183), and MechAir._steer brakes toward 16 m/s (mech_air.gd:29-31). I replayed both integrators at 120 Hz. With the AIR state, holding the stick, at 60-84 m/s, the feet are 0.07-0.67 m below the top when the capsule front reaches the face. With fall gravity and jet speed, as the solve assumes, they are +1.08 to +1.17 m above it. The rounded capsule then meets the edge at a normal of about 11-31 deg, which counts as floor, and MechContact.touchdown uses absf(_pre_vy) of a still-rising mech as the impact (mech_contact.gd:11). That gives a LAND recovery in most cases. The SLIDE hop survives only because AIR re-enters SLIDE (mech_air.gd:17-19).

## 5. [medium] game/player/mech/player_mech.gd:179 (clock)
**What**: Out of lens. During a local hit_pause, MechLocomotion.tick returns early (mech_locomotion.gd:81) and post_move returns early (mech_locomotion.gd:100). PlayerMech still runs move_and_slide with the unchanged, k-scaled velocity, so the 'frozen' mech keeps moving at full speed with no gravity. A touchdown inside the pause is never processed: on the next tick _on_floor is already true, so post_move's landing check never fires. The mech then stays in S.AIR ('fall') while standing on the ground: jump only sets _jump_buffer, which only _ground consumes, and there is no Events.landed or landing recovery. It stays stuck until a dash or boost, or until it leaves the floor again.

**Trigger**: Falling or diving at 20-45 m/s near the ground when a missile salvo's first hit lands. missile_manager.gd:198 calls Feel.local_hit_stop([player], 0.05), which is 6 ticks, about 1-2 m of fall. Also explosive or melee hits that pause the player as attacker (enemies/damage.gd:60), and defense/melee local stops. Jump-fire-land is ordinary play.

**Fix**: Do not freeze the body for every state: FEEL.md says player locomotion is never frozen, and juice section 2.2 says never freeze player-started boost, dash or landing. A 6-tick halt mid-assault on every salvo would read as a hitch. Honour _pause_t in tick() only while state is LOCKED or LUNGE, expose that as _loco.is_frozen(), and skip `velocity *= k; move_and_slide(); velocity /= k` only while it is true. Velocity is kept, or scaled by 0.05 for the FEEL.md attacker creep. Every other state ignores the pause. Also move post_move's touchdown and leave-floor detection above any pause early-return, so a contact change can never be dropped.

**Skeptic's check**: player_mech.gd:177-180 always runs move_and_slide. While paused, tick() returns at mech_locomotion.gd:81-83 and post_move() returns at :100-101, so the body keeps its stale velocity with no gravity. A floor contact made during the pause leaves _on_floor already true on the next tick (:79), so neither touchdown (:103) nor the LAND/GROUND transition ever runs. A mech in AIR or FLIGHT stays in S.AIR on the floor with move_state 'fall'. Triggers: missile_manager.gd:198 (0.05 s), defense_system.gd:264, melee_strike.gd:49, and enemies/damage.gd:60 (attacker creep pause). Minor inaccuracy: holding jump in AIR on the floor triggers the ascend in mech_air.gd:47-51, which lifts the mech off and lets it re-land, so jump is not completely dead.

## 6. [medium] game/player/mech/player_mech.gd:341 (clock)
**What**: PlayerMech.hit_pause() only forwards to _loco.hit_pause() and never to the model, and nothing else calls the hero model's hit_pause for the player (it has no signal hookups), so the hero AnimationTree keeps advancing on player_delta through every local hit-stop on the player. The model runs that tree in MANUAL mode precisely so hit_pause can freeze it and add the vibration (hero_model.gd:85-98).

**Trigger**: Every melee hit (local_hit_stop([player, receiver], 4-10 f)), perfect parry or salvo-hit pause: the enemy freezes and vibrates while the player's swing and pose play straight through the impact. CONTRACTS section 4 requires hit_pause to 'freeze own animation + motion, visual vibration'.

**Fix**: In PlayerMech.hit_pause also call `if _model != null and _model.has_method(&"hit_pause"): _model.call(&"hit_pause", seconds)`. If FEEL.md's 'attacker at 5 % speed' is the goal, give the model a creep scale instead of a full stop, but it must receive the pause either way.

**Skeptic's check**: player_mech.gd:341-342 forwards only to _loco. I grepped for hit_pause: nothing calls the hero model's hit_pause (hero_model.gd:179), and the model has no Events or Feel hookups. EnemyBase does forward its pause to its model (enemy_base.gd:157-158). As a result, the hero AnimationTree advances on player_delta (hero_model.gd:95-107) through every local stop on the player. That breaks CONTRACTS section 4 ('freeze own animation + motion') and the FEEL.md attacker creep, and the HeroModel hit_pause API listed in CONTRACTS section 5 is dead in game.

## 7. [low] game/player/mech/mech_air.gd:96 (clock)
**What**: The flight, ascend and hover EN drains (mech_air.gd:51, :54, :96) and the assault drain (mech_boost.gd:100) are charged as cost * delta, where delta is the locomotion (real) clock. MechEnergy.tick() runs regen, the regen delay and the Outage pause on Feel.player_delta, and CONTRACTS section 3 puts EN on player_delta. So in a global hit-stop the gauge keeps draining at the full real rate while regen and the Outage timer are frozen.

**Trigger**: Flying with the assault jet (120 EN/s) or ascending through the global stops kill_small/kill_large/hurt_heavy/boss_stagger (0.04-0.15 s at time_scale 0.05): each stop costs up to 18 EN instead of about 1, and in a kill streak (rate limit 3 stops/s) the effective drain is about 1.4x the tuned rate. Witch time is unaffected, since both clocks are real there.

**Fix**: Charge per-second EN costs on the player clock: `l.energy.drain(cost * l.pdelta)` in MechAir._vertical/flight and MechBoost.slide/assault, keeping the movement itself on rd. If paying on the movement clock is the deliberate choice, document it as an exception in CONTRACTS section 3.

**Skeptic's check**: The drains use the delta passed by tick(), which is rd = Feel.locomotion_delta (mech_locomotion.gd:88-90). These are mech_air.gd:51, :54 and :96, and mech_boost.gd:100. CONTRACTS section 3 lists EN/OD under Feel.player_delta, and MechEnergy.tick runs regen, the regen delay and the Outage timer on pd (player_mech.gd:171-173). In a non-witch global hit-stop, pd is about 0.05 x rd. So the gauge drains at the full real rate while regen and Outage are frozen: up to 120 EN/s x 0.15 s = 18 EN per boss_stagger stop during assault flight. The effect is small (low severity) but it does not follow the contract and is internally inconsistent.
